About the app

Nocturne is a calm breathing app for the end of the day, when thoughts keep spinning and sleep won't come. Your own breath moves it forward: keep your finger down and breathe in, the flame of a streetlamp grows; let go and breathe out, the light spreads over the street. As you walk, the village falls asleep, and the session ends on its own.

Help

How does a session work?

Tap “Take the path”. Keep a finger anywhere on the screen to breathe in: the flame of the streetlamp grows. Lift it to breathe out: the light spreads and you glide to the next lamp. One breath lights one streetlamp, all the way to your home. The short street takes about a minute and a half.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the gear icon next to the “Nocturne” title, then “Restore purchase”. The purchase also comes back on its own on any device signed in with the same Apple Account, and it is shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I save my sessions to Apple Health?

Tap the gear icon, then turn on “Save mindful minutes” and allow it in the Health prompt. Each finished path is saved as a mindful session.

I don't feel any vibration.

Check Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics. Vibrations are not available on iPad.

Nothing moves on the screen.

Reduce Motion is probably on (Settings › Accessibility › Motion). Nocturne then keeps the scene still on purpose.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Nocturne does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Your settings (breathing rhythm, street length) are stored only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Nocturne never sees your payment details.
  • Apple Health, only if you turn it on in the settings: each path is saved as a mindful session. Nocturne only writes this one type of data, never reads anything from Health, and the data stays in the Health app, under your control.
